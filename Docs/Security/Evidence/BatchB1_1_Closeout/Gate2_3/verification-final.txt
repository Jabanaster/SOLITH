## Root-cause finding (Phase 1-2)

The main-process IPC handlers for freeze propose/issue-consent/confirm
(`live-memory-freeze-propose`, `live-memory-freeze-issue-consent`,
`live-memory-freeze-start`) were ALREADY fully implemented and correct in
`electron/live-memory-ipc.ts` before this cycle began — they already enforced
trusted-sender validation, session ownership, feature-flag re-checks,
identity re-verification, and a real privileged consent dialog, and already
required `{proposalId, consentToken}` for confirmed start. The entire defect
was in the preload/renderer layer: `electron/preload.ts`'s
`liveMemoryFreezeStart` sent the legacy `{address, dataType, value,
intervalMs}` payload directly to the confirm-start channel (guaranteed schema
rejection), and no `liveMemoryFreezePropose`/`liveMemoryFreezeRequestConsent`
preload methods existed at all. See current-freeze-flow.md and
freeze-flow-gap-matrix.csv.
